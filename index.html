Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do these transaction benchmarks come from?

Our benchmarks are extracted from verified, registered property transaction deeds and statutory regulatory filings in Maharashtra. You receive certified agreement values rather than unverified portal listings or developer marketing brochures.

How is the "Estimated All-In Outlay" calculated?

While the Agreement Value is the exact statutory figure, we project your total cash requirement using the transparent formula: Agreement Value × 1.13. This incorporates 7% Maharashtra Stamp Duty + 5% GST on under-construction property + 1% statutory registration and local levies.

Will I receive calls from real estate brokers or agents?

Never. Homelytics is an independent transaction intelligence service, not a brokerage. We never sell, rent, or share buyer contact information with developers or channel partners.

Does this report replace an advocate's Title Search Report?

No. Our dossiers provide commercial transaction benchmarks, rate analyses, and regulatory litigation compilations. Buyers must always engage an independent property lawyer to conduct physical search reports at the sub-registrar office before executing final deeds.
